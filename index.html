The challenge

One trip. Four coupled systems. One blocked lane stalls them all.

Quay, transfer, yard and gate depend on each other, so a single stopped unit queues upstream and idles cranes.

Control and safety

Central flow, local safety veto

1 · Request

The vehicle asks for a segment and a time window.

2 · Reserve

The scheduler grants a non-overlapping slot and holds every other entry.

3 · Verify

Sensors confirm the vehicle, the container and the destination.

4 · Release

The segment clears before another unit may enter.

Request

Who acts: the vehicle.

During a lift the crane envelope stays closed. No software reservation overrides the physical safety state.
🛑

Stopped unit

Freeze, isolate, bypass, recover. Only the affected segment closes; nothing enters until clearance is verified.

🎟️

Double claim

One token, capacity 1. Tie-break: earliest reservation, priority, equipment ID, then timeout and re-plan.
